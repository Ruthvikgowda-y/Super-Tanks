# Super Tanks

**Free to play. Just open the link and have fun.**

Super Tanks is a turn-based artillery duel in the spirit of Pocket Tanks. Pick your angle, pick your power, pick your weapon, and try to out-shoot the bot across five worlds. No sign-up, no downloads, no ads — it's one HTML file that runs straight in your browser, on desktop or phone.

**▶ Play:** https://ruthvikgowda-y.github.io/Super-Tanks/

## Features
- You vs. a bot with 4 difficulty levels: Cadet, Veteran, Elite, Nightmare
- 25 weapons (Railgun, Singularity, Orbital Strike, Nanite Swarm, Tesla Coil, Antimatter Core…), each with its own icon, sound and musical motif
- 5 worlds with different gravity, destructible terrain and falling dirt
- Weapon draft, supply crates, hit streaks, adaptive synth soundtrack, motion blur

## Controls
| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Angle | ← → (Shift = ×5) |
| Power | ↑ ↓ (Shift = ×5) |
| Drive | A / D |
| Switch weapon | Q / E |
| Fire | Space / Enter |
| Pause · Sound · Music · Blur · Help | Esc · M · N · B · H |

You can also drag on the battlefield to aim.
